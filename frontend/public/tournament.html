Team Rosters
6 male + 4 female per team. Click any name to edit. Share link sends each team their fixtures.
Captain Submissions
Live feed of pairs submitted by team captains. Click "Use these pairs" to copy a submission into the Pair Builder.
Pair Builder
Each team fields 2 Men's Doubles, 2 Mixed Doubles, 1 Women's Doubles. No player can play more than one format.
Pick players from the dropdowns. Duplicates within the same team are highlighted in red. Pairs auto-save.
Men's Doubles
2 pools, round robin, each pair plays 3 matches (15 points). Top 2 from each pool advance to Knockouts.
Mixed Doubles
2 pools, round robin, each pair plays 3 matches (15 points). Top 2 from each pool advance to Knockouts.
Women's Doubles
All 4 teams in one round robin (15 points each). Top 2 go directly to the final.
Knockout Stage
Semis: 21 points · Final: best of 3 sets to 21.
